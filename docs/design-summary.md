# v1 design snapshot

Source of truth: the "Space Race Roguelike — Design Doc" in Claude Docs. This file is a snapshot.

## Pillars
1. Every choice costs something.
2. Abstract the engineering.
3. Crew are people.
4. Readable at a glance.
5. Focus first, expand later (content as data).

## v1 scope
- 6-month turns, 1957–1970, max 1 mission per turn: Budget, Plan, Build, Fly, News
- Milestones: satellite, crewed orbit, spacewalk, docking, lunar flyby, landing
- ~12 part cards; hand of 5; 3 slots (Booster / Upper / Payload); 2–3 synergies
- Visible reliability rolls; test flights and redundancy to reduce risk
- ~25 crisis events (~70% of missions get one), blue options from crew traits, tech/fuel, key personnel, favors
- 6 generated astronauts with traits; permadeath plus story-mode toggle
- Factions: Public + Congress (lose at zero)
- One Soviet rival on a fuzzy visible track
- Pick 1 of 3 rewards after milestones
- Hall of Fame
- One doctrine (the Saturn path), built swappable
- Plain boxes UI; CRT look in v2

## Parking lot
Sea Dragon / Nuclear / Mars / Spaceplane doctrines, more factions, rival intel, playable USSR and other nations, multiple rivals, deckbuilding, divergence events, animation/audio, Steam.
